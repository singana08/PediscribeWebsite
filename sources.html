Where the guidance comes from

Pediscribe places your records beside published public-health guides, for information only.

Purpose of Pediscribe

Pediscribe is a personal record-keeping tool. It helps parents and guardians keep track of their child's health information for their own reference. It does not give medical advice, diagnosis, treatment or recommendations, and nothing shown in it is a warning or an assessment of your child's health. Charts, ranges and schedules come from published public-health sources and are shown for general information only. Every decision about your child's health should be made with a qualified healthcare professional. In an emergency, call 112.

Not medical advice. Pediscribe is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any medical condition. Always consult a healthcare professional for medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. A number being "within the guide" does not mean your child is well. For any worry about your child, ask a doctor.

Pediscribe is an independent app. It is not affiliated with, or endorsed by, the WHO, the IAP, the CDC, the AAP, the ADA, the AASM, ACOG or any other body named on this page.

Growth charts

WHO Child Growth Standards (birth to 5 years: weight, length or height, head size), the IAP 2015 growth charts for Indian children (5 to 18 years: height and weight; Indian Academy of Pediatrics Growth Charts Committee, Indian Pediatrics 2015;52:47-55), and the WHO BMI-for-age tables (to 19 years). As IAP recommends, WHO is used below 5 years and IAP from 5. A percentile compares your child with healthy children of the same age and sex. The expected adult height uses the mid-parental method (Tanner): the parents' average height, plus 6.5 cm for a boy or minus 6.5 cm for a girl, give or take 8.5 cm.

Vaccines

The immunization schedule of the Indian Academy of Pediatrics (IAP) Advisory Committee on Vaccines and Immunization Practices (ACVIP), 2023 edition (published January 2024). A newer 2025 update exists and is not reflected yet, so the teenage vaccines are shown for information only. Vaccines are timed by actual age, not corrected age. Your doctor decides what is needed.

Sleep and screen time

WHO guidelines on physical activity, sedentary behaviour and sleep for children under 5 years (2019). From 5 to 18, sleep follows the American Academy of Sleep Medicine consensus on sleep duration (Paruthi et al., 2016), endorsed by the American Academy of Pediatrics. WHO gives no screen-time figure after 5, so none is shown.

Check-ups

Guidance from the IAP and from India's RBSK and Anemia Mukt Bharat programmes. Where there is no fixed advice, the app says the doctor decides.

Birth timing

Term and early birth follow ACOG Committee Opinion 579 and the WHO grouping of preterm birth. Growth charts use corrected age for a baby born before 37 weeks, until 2 years.
