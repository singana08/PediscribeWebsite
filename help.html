Questions and answers

The questions parents ask most. Tap a question to see the answer.

Privacy

Where are my records kept?

Only on this phone, encrypted. Pediscribe has no account, no access to the internet and no ads, so we never see your records.

Does Pediscribe need the internet?

No, it works fully offline. Only what you choose to do, like saving a backup to Google Drive or sending us an email, goes through other apps on your phone.

How do I stop others seeing my child's records?

Turn on App lock in Profile → Privacy and data: it asks for your fingerprint, face or phone PIN. Turn on "Hide details in reminders" so the lock screen never shows a name or a medicine.

Backups and phones

What if I lose my phone or uninstall the app?

Without a backup, the records cannot be recovered by anyone. Make a backup regularly in Profile → Backup and restore, and keep a copy off the phone, for example in Google Drive.

I forgot my backup password.

Nobody can open that backup, including us: the password is the only key. While you still have Pediscribe, make a new backup with a new password.

How do I move to a new phone?

Make a backup on the old phone. Install Pediscribe on the new one, go to Profile → Backup and restore, tap Restore and choose the backup, from the phone or from Google Drive, then enter its password.

Two parents, two phones?

Each parent makes a backup and shares it with the other. When restoring it, choose "Merge it with the records here instead", so both diaries become one.

Does it work on a tablet?

Yes. On an Android tablet held sideways, the menu moves to the left edge and the cards sit side by side, and the Diary keeps its calendar open. Held upright, it keeps the phone's menu. Your records stay on the device you use; to move them to a tablet, make a backup and restore it there.

Reminders

Why did a reminder not come?

Check that reminders are on in Profile and settings → Reminders, and that notifications are allowed for Pediscribe. Some phones (realme, OPPO, OnePlus, vivo, Xiaomi and others) stop apps that are swiped away: in Pediscribe's settings, under Battery, allow background activity and auto launch. The Reminders page has a button that opens it. See the steps for your phone.

Why is there an alarm icon at the top of my phone?

Medicine doses and your own reminders are set like an alarm so they come on time. The phone shows the alarm icon, and may show the time of the next one. Nothing about your child is shown.

Can I set a reminder for a class or an activity?

Yes. In Profile and settings → Reminders, under Your own, tap "Add a reminder". Give it a name, choose once, every day or some days, the time, and whether to be reminded 15 minutes or an hour before. They also show on Today, on Home, on their day.

Can I change the reminder sounds?

Yes. In Profile and settings → Reminders, under Sounds, tap ▶ to hear what each kind of reminder plays, and tap a row to choose another: any of Pediscribe's six sounds, or your phone's usual one. "Use for every reminder" gives them all the same sound.

How can I check that reminders work?

Open Profile and settings → Reminders and tap "Send a test reminder". Lock the phone to see how it looks there too.

Health information

Is Pediscribe medical advice?

No. Pediscribe is for your own records and reference. For any concern about your child, talk to your doctor. In an emergency, call 112.

Where do the growth charts and vaccine schedule come from?

Growth uses the WHO Child Growth Standards up to 5 years and the IAP 2015 charts from 5 to 18. Vaccines follow the IAP schedule. Sources and disclaimer has the details.

How do I show the records to a doctor?

Open Insights and tap "Complete medical history". It makes a report you can show on the phone, print or share. "Emergency card" has the essentials for an urgent visit.

Family

Can I add more than one child?

Yes. On Home, tap the child's name at the top to switch between children or add another.

How do I delete a child, or everything?

Profile → Delete (the child's name) erases only that child's records. Erase everything removes every child and record from this phone. Neither can be undone, so make a backup first if you may need them.
