Privacy policy

Private by design

How Pediscribe looks after your family's information. This is the same privacy notice you see in the app.

In short

  • Your child's records stay on this phone, encrypted.
  • No internet, no sign-up, no ads, no tracking.
  • We, the makers, never receive your data.
  • Nothing leaves the phone unless you share it.

Who we are

Pediscribe is a health diary for parents, made by an independent developer and published on Google Play under the developer name Pediscribe. It is not run by a hospital, a doctor or any health body.

This notice explains what happens to the information you put into Pediscribe. It applies to the app on your phone.

What the app holds

Only what you type in or choose:

  • Your child: name, date of birth, gender, blood group, allergies.
  • Health records: illnesses, temperatures, medicines and doses, doctor visits, vaccines, growth, check-ups, test results, hospital stays, conditions, food, sleep, screen time and notes.
  • Optional details: how your child was born, your name, relation and phone, the parents' heights (for the expected adult height), the doctor's name, clinic and phone, and family health history.
  • Photos, videos, sounds and documents you add, including your child's profile picture: prescriptions, reports, scans, and a first word, a laugh or first steps kept with a first. Each file is encrypted on its own (AES-256), with its key in the phone's secure keystore.
  • Your own reminders, if you make any: what each is for ("Music class"), when, and for which child.
  • Your settings: reminders, app lock, privacy choices, and when you agreed to this notice.
  • Notes about the app itself, kept on this phone: how many times each screen was opened, and short technical notes about recent errors (where in the app's code they happened, never what you typed).

What we never collect

Pediscribe has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere. There is no account and no sign-in.

It does not use your location, contacts, camera, call log, messages or your phone's files, and it does not ask for those permissions. The microphone is used only while you record a sound for a first, and the recording stays on this phone.

There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and nothing is reported from inside the app on its own.

The makers of Pediscribe never receive your child's information, so we cannot see it, sell it or share it.

Where your information is stored, and how it is protected

Everything stays on this phone, in an encrypted database (AES-256, using SQLCipher). The key to it is stored in the phone's secure keystore, never next to the data.

App lock asks for your fingerprint, face or phone PIN. That check is done by Android: Pediscribe never sees your fingerprint or face.

Hide details in reminders (on by default) makes reminders that name your child or a medicine say only "Pediscribe reminder". Hide from screenshots keeps the app out of the recent-apps preview and blocks screenshots.

Android's automatic cloud backup is switched off for Pediscribe, so the phone does not copy the records to your Google account by itself.

When anything leaves the phone

Only when you choose to share or save something:

  • A backup file: locked with a password you choose (AES-256-GCM, with the key made from your password by PBKDF2), unless you choose not to lock it. Without the password, nobody can read a locked backup, including us. Keep the password safe: it cannot be recovered. A backup you choose not to lock can be read by anyone who has the file. Videos go into a backup only if you choose to include them.
  • A medical history or emergency card (PDF): this is not locked. Share it only with people you trust. Phone numbers are added to a report only if you switch them on.
  • A document or photo you share from the vault: the shared copy is not locked either, and the app says so before sharing.
  • A backup folder on this phone, if you choose one: backups are saved there, locked with your password if you chose one, and older ones beyond the number you choose are removed. Pediscribe can reach that one folder only, because you allowed it, and never touches other files in it.
  • A message to us (feedback, an idea, a problem report): it opens in your own email app and is sent only if you send it. A problem report adds the app version, the phone model, the Android version and the technical notes about recent errors, never your child's records. If you tick "Help improve Pediscribe", a message also includes how many times each screen was opened. You see all of it in the email before sending. We use what you write only to reply to you and to improve the app.
  • A request to subscribe to Pediscribe news by email, only if you choose it in Profile and tick to agree: it opens in your own email app and is sent only if you send it. We use your email address only to send occasional news about Pediscribe, never anything about your child, and never share or sell it. Unsubscribe at any time from the same place, or by replying to any email, and we delete your address.

Where a shared file goes (Google Drive, WhatsApp, email, a folder) is then up to you and that service's own privacy rules.

Permissions the app asks for, and why

  • Fingerprint or face: for the app lock.
  • Notifications: for the reminders you switch on (doses, visits, vaccines, check-ups).
  • Run at start-up: to put the reminders back after the phone restarts.
  • Alarms and reminders: so a reminder comes at the time you set, not up to an hour later. Medicine doses and your own reminders are set like an alarm, so your phone may show an alarm icon at the top.
  • Vibration: so a reminder can buzz.
  • Microphone: only to record a sound for a first, when you tap Record. Android asks you the first time.

Nothing else. Photos and videos are taken with your phone's own camera app. Files are chosen one at a time through Android's own file picker, and a backup folder through Android's own folder screen, with your permission for that folder only. So Pediscribe needs no camera or storage permission.

Children's information and consent

Pediscribe is meant to be used by a parent or legal guardian, aged 18 or older, about their own child. Children should not use it themselves.

Before any information is entered, you are asked to confirm that you are the parent or guardian and that you agree to this notice. Your agreement is recorded on this phone only, with its date.

Health information about a child is sensitive. Please use the app lock, and think before sharing reports.

Your choices

  • See and change any record at any time.
  • Delete a single record, or a child with all their records (Profile).
  • Erase everything on this phone (Profile → Erase everything).
  • Take your records with you: a backup file, or a medical history PDF.
  • Switch reminders, the app lock and the privacy options on or off (Profile).

How long information is stored

Until you delete it. Uninstalling the app deletes everything it holds, immediately and permanently.

Backup files you saved stay wherever you saved them until you delete them there. If you remove the app without a backup, the records cannot be recovered by anyone.

The limits of any protection

No phone is perfectly safe. Someone who can unlock your phone, a phone that has been "rooted", or harmful apps on it can weaken these protections.

Once you share a report, it is no longer protected by the app.

Purpose of the app, and not medical advice

Pediscribe is a personal record-keeping tool. It helps parents and guardians keep track of their child's health information for their own reference. It does not give medical advice, diagnosis, treatment or recommendations, and nothing shown in it is a warning or an assessment of your child's health. Charts, ranges and schedules come from published public-health sources and are shown for general information only. Every decision about your child's health should be made with a qualified healthcare professional. In an emergency, call 112.

Pediscribe is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any medical condition. Always consult a healthcare professional for medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Guidance shown in the app comes from the WHO, the IAP and others, for information only (see Sources and disclaimer).

Changes to this notice

If this notice changes, the app will show the new version and ask you to agree again before you continue.

Our website

Our website, pediscribe.vercel.app, has no cookies, analytics, advertising or tracking, and no forms. Its font, icons and pictures are stored on the site itself, so visiting it does not contact any other service.

It is hosted by Vercel, which, like any web host, processes technical information such as your IP address to deliver the pages and keep the service secure.

If you choose light or dark on the website, that choice is kept only in your own browser.

The app opens the website only when you tap a link to it, such as "Read it online" on this notice. It opens in your browser, and the app sends nothing with it.

Contact

Questions about privacy: use Profile and settings → Write to us in the app, or write to primarydeveloper.apps@gmail.com.
