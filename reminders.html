Getting reminders on time

Pediscribe sets each reminder on your phone ahead of time. Some phones stop apps that are swiped away, to save battery, and hold their reminders back. One setting, done once, fixes it.

Why this happens. Phone makers such as realme, OPPO, OnePlus, vivo and Xiaomi add their own battery saver. When an app is swiped away from the recent-apps screen, it can be paused until you open it again. Apps the maker already knows, like messaging apps, are let through; others need your permission once.

Step 1 · In Pediscribe

Open Profile and settings → Reminders. Pediscribe shows a yellow card only when something needs your attention:

  • Notifications are off for Pediscribe: tap Turn on.
  • Reminders may come late: tap Allow, so reminders can come at the exact minute.
  • Reminders when Pediscribe is closed (on phones that pause apps): tap Open. It takes you straight to Pediscribe's page in your phone's settings, for step 2.

While you are there, choose a sound for each kind of reminder under Sounds, so you can tell a dose from a vaccine without looking at the phone.

Step 2 · In your phone's settings

On Pediscribe's page in the phone's settings (or Settings → Apps → Pediscribe), find your phone below. Menu names differ between models and Android versions: if you cannot find one, search Settings for background, battery or auto launch.

realme, OPPO and OnePlus

  1. Tap Battery usage (or Battery).
  2. Turn on Allow background activity.
  3. Turn on Allow auto launch, if you see it.

vivo and iQOO

  1. Tap Battery.
  2. Choose Allow high background power consumption (on some models: Settings → Battery → Background power consumption management → Pediscribe → Allow).
  3. Turn on Auto-start, if you see it.

Xiaomi, Redmi and POCO

  1. Turn on Autostart.
  2. Tap Battery saver and choose No restrictions.

Samsung

  1. Tap Battery and choose Unrestricted.
  2. In Settings → Battery → Background usage limits, make sure Pediscribe is not in Sleeping apps or Deep sleeping apps.

Huawei and Honor

  1. Go to Settings → Battery → App launch.
  2. Find Pediscribe and switch to Manage manually.
  3. Turn on Auto-launch, Secondary launch and Run in background.

Google Pixel, Motorola, Nokia and others

  1. Usually nothing is needed.
  2. If reminders still come late, tap Battery (or App battery usage) and choose Unrestricted.

Step 3 · Check that it works

In Pediscribe, open Profile and settings → Reminders and tap Send a test reminder. Lock the phone to see how it looks there too.

For a real test, add a medicine with a reminder a few minutes ahead, swipe Pediscribe away from the recent-apps screen, and wait for it.

About the alarm icon

Medicine doses and your own reminders are set like an alarm, so they come on time. Your phone may show an alarm icon at the top, and may show the time of the next one. Nothing about your child is shown.

Reminders are a help, not a guarantee: a phone can still delay them to save battery.
